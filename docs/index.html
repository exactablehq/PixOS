The Pixel 1 has 32GB or 128GB of internal storage. PixOS queries Pixel free space, transfers in smart 5GB batches, and pauses if storage is low so Google Photos has time to upload.

Get Started in 3 Simple Steps

No command line or technical knowledge required. Drag the app to Applications and follow the on-screen prompts.

01

Connect Your iPhone

Plug your iPhone into your Mac via Lightning or USB-C. Unlock the screen and tap "Trust This Computer". PixOS automatically detects your camera roll.

02

Connect Google Pixel

On your Pixel, enable Developer Options and toggle on USB Debugging. Plug it in via USB or pair via Wireless ADB. Bundled ADB handles the rest.

03

Click "Start Sync to Pixel"

PixOS transfers your media to /sdcard/DCIM/iPhone/, triggers Android's MediaScanner, and Google Photos starts uploading original quality backups immediately!

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Google Pixel unlimited backup and transferring from iOS.

Why does the original 2016 Google Pixel have unlimited storage?
When Google released the original Pixel (OG Pixel 2016, "sailfish" / "marlin"), they promised lifetime unlimited original quality Google Photos cloud backups for life for any photo or video backed up from that physical device. That promise remains active today!
Will my iPhone photos and 4K videos be compressed?
No. PixOS transfers the original lossless master media directly from your iPhone (including 4K 60fps HDR, ProRAW DNG, and ProRes). When uploaded via the OG Pixel, Google Photos uploads them at full Original Quality with zero compression.
How does PixOS handle Live Photos?
PixOS automatically pairs the still image (.HEIC / .JPG) with the companion 3-second QuickTime video (.MOV) and injects standardized Google Camera Motion Photo XMP container metadata. Google Photos displays and plays it as a native Motion Photo.
Do I need to install ADB or Android Studio manually?
No! PixOS comes with a prepackaged ADB binary inside the application bundle. You do not need Homebrew, Android Studio, or any terminal setup.
Can I connect my Google Pixel over Wi-Fi instead of USB cable?
Yes! PixOS features full Dual-Mode ADB. You can connect your Pixel over your local home Wi-Fi network using Wireless ADB, while keeping only your iPhone connected via USB cable.
